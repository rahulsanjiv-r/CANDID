# CANDID Trademark Policy

## What is trademarked

The following are trademarks of the CANDID project maintainers:

- The name "CANDID" when used in connection with dark-pattern auditing or consumer interface testing
- The full expansion "Consumer Audits of Nudges & Deceptive Interface Design"
- The CANDID logo (when created)
- The CANDID report card format and visual design
- The phrase "The company being judged should never be the one writing the report" when used in connection with CANDID

## What this means

### You CAN:

- ✅ The CANDID source code is licensed under AGPL-3.0. You can use, modify, and distribute the code freely as long as you follow the AGPL-3.0 terms.
- ✅ You can say "based on CANDID," "compatible with CANDID," "uses CANDID's methodology," or "forked from CANDID" in truthful, descriptive statements.
- ✅ Linking to the official CANDID project is permitted.
- ✅ Academic papers can reference CANDID by name with proper citation.
- ✅ Internal testing within your organization is allowed.

### You CANNOT (without written permission):

- ❌ Naming your own product, service, or project CANDID is prohibited. If you fork the code and offer it as a service, use a different name.
- ❌ Displaying the CANDID logo on your own website, product, or marketing materials requires permission.
- ❌ Publishing "CANDID reports" or "CANDID-certified" results is only allowed if you use the official, unmodified CANDID test suite and follow the full publishing process. This includes the right-of-reply period.
- ❌ Suggesting that the CANDID project or its maintainers endorse your product, service, or fork is not permitted.
- ❌ Registering the CANDID name as a trademark, domain name, or social media handle in any jurisdiction is forbidden.

## Why we do this

CANDID's value depends on trust. If anyone could publish a "CANDID Report" using modified tests or without the right-of-reply process, companies and consumers would lose faith in the findings. The trademark ensures that:

1. Every report bearing the CANDID name was produced by the official, unmodified methodology.
2. No one can publish inaccurate results under the CANDID brand.
3. The project's reputation remains tied to the quality of its actual work.

This is the same model used by Firefox, Kubernetes, and WordPress.

We enforce this policy in good faith. We start with a private request to resolve the issue before taking further action. Our goal is to protect the integrity of CANDID's findings.
